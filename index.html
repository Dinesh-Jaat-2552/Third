Genetic Screening and
Precision Medicine

At Kolkata Gynae & Onco Clinic, we believe every woman’s cancer journey is
unique. That’s why we offer Genetic Screening and Precision Medicine in
Kolkata, a personalised approach that identifies genetic risks, predicts cancer
behaviour, and tailors treatment for the best outcomes. By combining advanced
genetic testing with targeted therapies, we empower patients with proactive
care, early interventions, and treatment strategies designed just for them.

Unique Micro-Surgeries by Dr Arunava Roy

Robotic Cytoreductive Surgery for Advanced
Ovarian Cancer

Dr. Arunava Roy specialises in performing cytoreductive surgery for
advanced ovarian cancer using state-of-the-art robotic technology. His
expertise allows for the precise removal of tumour deposits with minimal complications, reduced recovery time, and improved long-term outcomes, offering patients a safer and more effective treatment
pathway.

Robotic Groin node dissection

Dr. Arunava Roy is experienced in performing robotic groin node
dissections for gynaecologic cancers. Using advanced robotic
technology, he ensures precise lymph node removal with less pain, faster recovery, and improved surgical accuracy compared to traditional methods.
